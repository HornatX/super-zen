# SuperZen - 定制禅模式 (SuperZen - Custom Zen Mode)

A highly immersive, distraction-free writing environment for Obsidian. Perfect for creative writers, novelists, and long-form content creators.

这是一个专为小说创作者、撰稿人和长文写作者打造的 **极简浸入式写作环境（禅模式）**。它能够彻底隐去所有非写作界面的视觉噪音（如边栏、状态栏、标题、元数据等），帮助您将注意力百分之百聚焦于文字本身。

---

## 🌟 Key Features (核心特色)

### 1. Custom Zen Mode (一键进入专注空间)
- **Hide UI Clutter**: Instantly hide sidebars, status bar, ribbons, tab headers, document metadata, and drag resize handles.
- **Auto-Fullscreen**: Trigger standard HTML5 full-screen mode automatically upon activation to block system-level distractions.
- **Focus Indicator**: Highlight only your active editor while everything else fades away.

### 2. Smart Dual-Pane Comparison (智能多窗格双屏对照)
- Keep your outline, draft, or reference material visible on the left split, while your main writing area on the right enters a dedicated full-screen focus state.
- Supports quick hotkey switching between **"Single-Pane Full Screen" (单屏独占)** and **"Dual-Pane Comparison" (双屏对照)**.

### 3. Floating Vertical Tabs (极简悬浮选项卡)
- Transform horizontal browser-like tabs into ultra-minimal, vertically oriented text labels pinned to the right screen edge.
- **Ghost State**: Inactive tabs remain semi-transparent to minimize visual footprint.
- **Active Glow**: Selected tabs illuminate with an accent color line indicator without expanding or squeezing your text container.

### 4. Custom Margin Padding (Base 留白机制)
- Automatically centers text and adds elegant margins (`15vw` padding) when opening dedicated `.base` drafts, providing a typewriter-like aesthetic.

## 🛠️ How to Use (如何使用)

### 1. Commands & Hotkeys (常用快捷命令)
Open your Obsidian Command Palette (`Ctrl + P` or `Cmd + P`) and search:
- **`开启/关闭 定制禅模式`** *(Toggle Super Zen Mode)*: Instantly enter or exit full screen and hide UI elements.
- **`切换 单屏/双屏 对照模式`** *(Toggle Single/Dual Pane Mode)*: Switch layout behaviors on the fly.

> 💡 **Tip (建议)**: We highly recommend binding a simple hotkey (like `F11` or `Alt + Z`) to **Toggle Super Zen Mode** for seamless immersion.

### 2. Settings (设置面板)
- **智能多窗格对照 (Keep Dual Panes)**: Keep reference panes on the left pane while typing in Zen mode on the right.
- **极简悬浮选项卡 (Vertical Tabs)**: Strip tab borders, icons, and close buttons into elegant, border-pinned floating vertical text.
- **固定右分屏标签 (Fixed Right Tabs)**: Lock the floating tab labels to the rightmost pane in dual-pane layout regardless of active editor focus.

## 🚀 Installation (安装)

Once approved on the community store, you can install it directly within Obsidian:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Community Plugins** > **Browse**.
2. Search for **`SuperZen`** (or **`定制禅模式`**).
3. Click **Install** and **Enable**.
